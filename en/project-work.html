Deliverables

What your team takes away

Design assets and documentation to validate the solution, move into development and keep improving the product.

We agree on deliverables to fit the task. A small product area does not need every artifact of a large project.

  • User flows and interaction logic

    Primary and alternative paths, connections between screens, and interface behavior.

  • Screen designs and states

    The required screens and responsive layouts, including loading, empty, error and access-restricted states.

  • Prototype and components

    An interactive prototype to test open design questions, a UI kit and guidance on using it.

  • Developer handoff

    Design walkthroughs with developers and a review of the implementation within the agreed scope.

Working together

How we work

  1. 01

    Understand the task

    We review the product, its users, constraints and available materials.

  2. 02

    Agree on the scope

    We define deliverables, milestones, cost, timeline and how reviews will work.

  3. 03

    Validate the direction

    We review the user flow and key screens before moving into detailed design.

  4. 04

    Hand off and review

    I walk the team through the design. We agree on the scope of implementation support.

If the task is still too broad, we identify a self-contained first phase worth delivering on its own.

Can we start without a detailed brief?

Yes. Send a description and whatever you already have: a product, designs or a list of requirements. We will identify what needs clarifying before an estimate and decide where to start.

Can you work on just part of the product?

Yes, as long as we can define its boundaries and dependencies. I work with existing interfaces and components and account for your development team’s constraints.

Is development included?

Design and development are separate stages. A browser prototype or limited frontend work can be discussed separately after reviewing your stack and API. Building the entire service is not included in a design project.

How do you estimate cost and timeline?

After reviewing the task and agreeing on the outcome. Before work starts, we confirm the scope, milestones, payment terms and review process. Additional tasks are estimated separately.
