# Article / Case Digest -- Polina Tsoy

Proof points for evaluations. Metrics only as stated in portfolio / CV — do not invent.

## WakeupWarrior (WUW)

- **URL:** https://neolissa.github.io/polina-tsoy-designer/en/wuw.html
- **App:** https://app.wakeupwarrior.com/
- **Context:** Personal-growth platform, 5000+ users
- **Role:** Lead Product Designer (research → development)
- **Problem:** 200+ styles, slow delivery, no White Label
- **Solution:** 35 reusable components, tokens, evolutionary refactor; constructor for ~80% of UI
- **Results:** −90% styles, ×3 development speed, White Label opened, consistency improved
- **Article:** https://vc.ru/design/717548

## TVIP

- **URL:** https://neolissa.github.io/polina-tsoy-designer/en/tvip.html
- **Context:** Media service, ready backend, outdated UI, 5 platforms
- **Role:** Product Designer
- **Solution:** Profiles for 4 personas, day/night themes, UI kit for 5 platforms, onboarding
- **Results:** Personalized profiles, auto theme switch, consistent multi-device experience

## Relaunch (Relax.coin / internal platform)

- **URL:** https://neolissa.github.io/polina-tsoy-designer/en/relaunch.html
- **Context:** Corporate employee platform, 100% of company staff
- **Role:** Product Designer (lead)
- **Problem:** 5+ fragmented tools, outdated UI, no social/gamification
- **Solution:** Unified ecosystem 25+ features, design grooming, DS 50+ components
- **Results:** ×4 session time, +40% engagement, 60% mobile activity, 100% adoption

## DocsBird

- **URL:** https://neolissa.github.io/polina-tsoy-designer/en/docsbird.html
- **Context:** International document management (Europe & Baltics)
- **Role:** Product Designer (lead)
- **Problem:** Launch from scratch in 1 year under regulatory and cultural constraints
- **Solution:** Separate brand + design system; registration 7 → 3 steps; self-service over complex B2B flows
- **Results:** Launch in 4 countries, +45% conversion, trustworthiness feedback

## Positioning themes (for framing, not new claims)

- Pragmatic design over trend-chasing
- Design as part of the development loop
- Research/data over “like/dislike”
- Design systems as a team asset, not a component dump
