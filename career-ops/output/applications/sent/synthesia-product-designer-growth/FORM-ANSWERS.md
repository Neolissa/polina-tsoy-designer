## Tell us about a time you disagreed with a product problem, not just the solution

```
On DocsBird (international document product), the brief framed registration as a compliance data-capture problem: collect every regulatory field upfront so “we’re safe.” That produced a 7-step signup and treated activation as paperwork completeness.

I disagreed with the problem framing. The real job was time-to-value under cultural and regulatory constraints — users needed trust and a working product path, not a front-loaded form. I pushed progressive disclosure and self-service: keep required checks, but don’t make activation equal “fill everything first.”

We cut registration from 7 steps to 3, launched in 4 countries, and conversion rose +45%. Reflection: I would have mapped mandatory vs deferrable compliance fields in writing with legal/PM even earlier, so the reframing wasn’t a late debate.
```
